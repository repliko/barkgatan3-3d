# Deploy Mobile 3D, AR & Interior Walkthrough Bundle in 10 Seconds (Free)

1. **Netlify Drop (Fastest — Zero Account Setup Needed to Preview):** Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser and drag-and-drop this entire `web_viewer` folder onto the page to get an instant `https://...netlify.app` link.
2. **GitHub Pages (Permanent Free Hosting):** Push `exports/web_viewer` to a GitHub repo and enable **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch** (HTTPS is required by iOS Quick Look & Android WebXR).
3. **Open on Phone or Desktop:**
   - `index.html`: Toggle side-by-side between **45,0° Sadeltak** and **30,0° Sadeltak** and tap **"Visa i 1:1 AR"** on iPhone (`.usdz` Quick Look) or Android (`.glb` Scene Viewer).
   - `walkthrough.html`: Step inside at `1.65 m` eye level with realistic procedural PBR brick/oak/limestone textures, live ceiling switcher (`45° Open` / `45° Scissor` / `30° Open`), Day/Evening lighting, and interactive doors!
